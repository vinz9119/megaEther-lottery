This repository contains several independent blockchain engineering prototypes and research notes.

## Projects

### Sui Escrow
Buyer/seller escrow lifecycle, payment validation, settlement design, security notes, and roadmap.

### Sui Guestbook
Shared-object state and per-address message storage.

### Sui Token Vault
Design-stage asset accounting and authorization model.

### Crypto Risk Notes
Engineering-focused protocol risk review checklists.

## Development standard

Projects are labeled honestly as prototypes or research until they are compiled, tested, and reviewed. No project claims production deployment without evidence.
