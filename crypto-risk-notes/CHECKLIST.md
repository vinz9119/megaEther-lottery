# Protocol Review Checklist

## Contract
- What can an administrator change?
- Can contracts be upgraded?
- Which functions can move user funds?
- Are emergency controls documented?

## Market
- What liquidity is available?
- What assumptions are made about price feeds?
- What happens during extreme volatility?

## Operational
- Are keys multisig?
- Are contracts independently audited?
- Is there a bug bounty?
- Are deployments reproducible?

## User
- What can cause permanent loss?
- Which risks are borne by the user?
- What happens if an external dependency fails?
